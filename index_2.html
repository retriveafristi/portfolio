About

I grew up answering “where are you from?” with a shrug: Hong Kong, Madrid, Jakarta, pick one.

It meant I never had a fixed idea of what “normal” sounds like, and that's turned out to be the actual job: the line that lands with a marketing director in London can fall flat with a rider in Jakarta, and no amount of polish fixes that if you skip the translation.

I've spent my career sitting between product, sales, community and customers, turning what a team builds and what a customer actually feels into a story sales can repeat and customers can trust.

That's meant building go-to-market for mobility features at GoTo Group in Jakarta, and growing subscriptions and flagship events for The Alliance across North America and Europe.

Product launch or brand campaign, the method doesn't change: find out what's actually true for the customer, build the narrative and enablement that gets the rest of the business saying the same thing, then let the numbers tell me what to fix next.
